## Full description

Geminos Twitch Title Generator is a free browser extension that gives Twitch streamers fresh title ideas in seconds. No sign-up, no API keys, no data collection. Just open the popup, type your game, pick a stream style and goal, and hit Generate.

Every title is built from a local engine with hundreds of phrase combinations, so you get varied results each time. Titles adapt to your selected style (competitive, chill, funny, ranked, casual, educational, challenge, community, speedrun, or first playthrough) and your session goal (rank up, grinding, learning, viewer games, speedrun, and more).

You can toggle emoji on or off, generate batches of 5 or 10, and copy individual titles or all of them at once. The extension runs entirely inside your browser with no network requests, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Geminos Twitch Title Generator works on both Firefox and Chrome. It requests zero special permissions and stores nothing on your machine.

Built by Geminos as a free tool for the streaming community.

## Features

- Generates creative Twitch stream titles locally

- Generate 5 or 10 titles per batch
- One-click copy (single or all)

- No data collection, no analytics
- Zero browser permissions
- Works on Firefox and Chrome

Geminos Twitch Title Generator is a lightweight, free browser extension for Twitch streamers who want quick title inspiration. Enter your game or category, select a stream style (competitive, chill, funny, ranked, and more), choose a session goal, and hit Generate. The extension produces 5 or 10 unique titles per batch using a local phrase engine with hundreds of combinations. Copy individual titles or the whole batch with one click. ### ~200 words

Coming up with a good Twitch stream title before every session gets old fast. Geminos Twitch Title Generator takes the friction out of it. This free browser extension lets you type in your game or category, pick from 10 stream styles (competitive, chill, funny, ranked, casual, educational, challenge, community, speedrun, first playthrough), and select a session goal (rank up, grinding, viewer games, speedrun, and more). Hit Generate and get 5 or 10 unique title suggestions instantly.

Titles are assembled locally from a phrase engine with varied templates, openers, style-specific phrases, goal phrases, separators, and optional emoji. The combinations run into the thousands, so you get different results every time. Each title card shows the character count, and a Copy button puts the title on your clipboard with one click. Copy All grabs the whole batch.

The extension is extremely lightweight. It uses no API, no backend, no database, no login, and no analytics. It makes zero network requests and collects zero data. Permissions are minimal — it only needs the popup window. It works on Firefox and Chrome with identical functionality.

Built and maintained by Geminos. Free for every streamer.

# Privacy Policy — Geminos Twitch Title Generator

**Last updated:** August 2026

## Summary

This extension collects **zero** user data. All title generation runs locally inside your browser. Nothing leaves your machine.

## Data collection

- No personal data is collected.
- No browsing activity is tracked.
- No analytics or telemetry are used.
- No title data is sent to any server.
- No cookies are set.
- No network requests are made.

## Permissions

The extension requests no special browser permissions. It operates entirely within its popup window.

## Third parties

No third-party services, APIs, or scripts are used.
